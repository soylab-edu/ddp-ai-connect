# B — 열린 자리

- Flow: automation. Storyboard approval is not required; production and rendering are authorized.
- Output: responsive standalone header, silent 14-second single-play composition, desktop MP4, final poster, mobile evidence.
- Purpose: establish NEXT K-CORE AI CONNECT as a place where people meet through AI. SOYLAB makes the place; AI remains a small mediator.
- Fixed from the first frame: event identity, SOYLAB, date, venue, event character, program and visitor-guide links.
- Main copy: AI를 사이에 두고, 사람과 사람이 만나다.
- Source constraints: game/art/story are the three creative genres. Exhibition/screening/lecture/exchange are activities. Dates and visitor times are supplied planning values, not independently verified public information.
- Controls: single play, final hold, replay, pause, reduced-motion final frame. No audio.

## 14-second direction

| Time | Form | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 0–3.1 | Two purple curved bands open a common space | Two people, with a small AI mediator |
| 3.1–6.5 | Curved bands repeat as three related shapes on purple | 게임·예술·이야기, three creative genres |
| 6.5–9.8 | The same opposing curves form an open place on lime | 함께, a place to meet |
| 9.8–12 | Curves settle around the final message | AI를 사이에 두고, 사람과 사람이 만나다. |
| 12–14 | Final poster holds | Read and choose a destination |
