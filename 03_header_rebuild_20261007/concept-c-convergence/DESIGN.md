# C — CONVERGENCE

Small individual colors gather into one expressive word, then release into shared language. A continuous 3D stage carries the dot globe, red dimensional word, local paint, black reflective expansion and explosion before eight typography rows take over. The final two rows remain below the event poster and continue moving in the web header.

The black field is #111111. Core colors: SOYLAB red #DF0221, purple #7A00EE, neon #CCFF00, and white #F5F5F5. Fine pastel dots and paint use coral #FFC4CD, lavender #C9B9FF, mint #B4EDD7, blue #BFD7FF and pale yellow #F1EDB3. Satin dots have no starbursts or toy-ball shading. The word has deep bevelled geometry, broad white panel reflections, purple and neon rim reflections, and restrained roughness. Black remains visibly black with illuminated shape.

Outfit 800 supplies the large extruded word. Outfit 600–700 and IBM Plex Sans KR 600 supply metadata and final text. A few corner labels preserve stage space; no opaque top/bottom rails confine the motion. Final dates/location and navigation remain readable. The real supplied SOY.LAB mark appears unmodified at the explosion peak.
