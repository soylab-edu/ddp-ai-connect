# AI CONNECT / kinetic header

Concept: separate people and AI become one continuous, living line; oversized supplied typography punctuates the journey.

User brand truth: purple #8000ef, neon #ccff00, near-black #101010, white. Original transparent PNG words are the focal type; preserve the supplied files. Supporting type uses bundled Barlow Condensed with system sans-serif Korean fallback. No sound. Canvas 1440 × 760, a finite 24-second Hyperframes timeline, repeated only by the website player.

Focal: huge typography; edge anchors: NEXT K-CORE, event date, serial scene number. Supporting detail: articulated people and AI continually walking forward along a morphing path. Background: flat brand fields, hard graphic circles and push/iris transitions. No photography, glass, gradients or shadows.

Sequence: purple AI CONNECT → neon AI × HUMAN → black mask typography → purple people/circular path → purple closing title returning to opening. Scene entrances use power4, back and expo easing. Walk geometry is sampled deterministically into SVG attribute keyframes. Stance feet remain at fixed path parameters while swing feet advance; the path itself morphs under them. Wraps occur beyond the canvas on the open line.
